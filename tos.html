5. Community conduct

FemboyMC is strictly SFW. While on the Server you agree not to:

  • Harass, bully, threaten, or target anyone, including for their identity, gender, sexuality, race, religion, or disability.
  • Fetishize or sexualize other players or the femboy and LGBTQ+ community.

6. Fair play

Keep things fair for everyone. You may not:

  • Use hacked clients, cheats, macros, auto-clickers, or mods that give an unfair advantage (for example X-ray, kill aura, fly, or auto-mine).
  • Exploit bugs, glitches, or duplication methods. If you find one, report it to staff. Responsible reports may be rewarded.

  • Attack, overload, or try to crash the Server, or try to get access to systems or accounts you shouldn't have.
  • Trade in-game items or currency for real money or for items on other servers.

Client-side mods that don't give an advantage (like performance mods, minimaps without cave or entity radar, and shaders) are generally fine. Ask staff if you're unsure.
