1. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how FemboyMC ("we", "us"), the Minecraft server network run by the Femboy Hangout community, collects and uses information when you play on our servers, use our related Discord features, or visit this website.

It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

2. What we collect

Collected automatically when you join

DataWhy
Minecraft username and UUIDTo identify your account, save your progress, and apply ranks or punishments.
IP addressTo connect you to the server, detect alt accounts and ban evasion, and protect against attacks.
Chat messages, private messages, and commandsTo moderate the server and investigate reports.
Gameplay data (inventories, builds, block changes, balances, ranks, stats, locations)To run the gamemodes, roll back griefing, and investigate cheating or exploits.
Connection info (join and leave times, playtime, Minecraft version, client brand)For server stats, troubleshooting, and anti-cheat.

Information you give us

  • Reports, tickets, and appeals: whatever you include, such as your Discord username and any screenshots or details.
  • Discord account linking (if offered): your Discord user ID and username, linked to your Minecraft account.
  • Donations: if you donate through Ko-fi, Ko-fi tells us your display name, the amount, any message you add, and possibly your email address. We never see your card or bank details.

We don't ask for or want sensitive information like your real name, address, or identity details. Please don't share them in-game.

3. How we use it

  • To run the Server and save your progress.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and rules, including investigating reports, catching cheaters, and stopping ban evasion.
  • To protect the Server and players from attacks, abuse, and fraud.
  • To give donation perks and keep basic donation records.
  • To understand how the Server is used (for example, peak player counts) so we can improve it.
  • To respond to you when you contact us.

We don't sell your data, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't build profiles about you beyond what's needed for moderation.

4. Legal basis (EU/UK players)

If you're in the EU, EEA, or UK, we rely on these legal bases under the GDPR:

  • Contract: to provide the Server you've chosen to join.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the Server safe, moderate it, prevent cheating, and improve it.
  • Legal obligation: where we need to keep or share data to comply with the law.
  • Consent: for optional features like Discord linking. You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. Who we share it with

Only FemboyMC staff who need the data to do their job can access it. We only share data with:

  • Service providers that help us run things, such as our server host, Cloudflare (website hosting and DDoS protection), Discord, and Ko-fi. They only process data as needed to provide their service.
  • Mojang/Microsoft, who handle Minecraft login and authentication under their own privacy policy.
  • Other players see what any Minecraft server shows publicly, like your username, skin, public chat, and builds.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it, or if we believe it's needed to protect someone's safety.

Some of these providers may store data outside your country. Where that happens, we rely on their standard safeguards for international transfers.

6. How long we keep it

  • Gameplay data and progress: for as long as the relevant world or gamemode exists, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Chat and command logs: usually around 90 days, unless they're needed for an open report or punishment.
  • IP addresses and connection logs: usually around 90 days. For banned players, we keep them for as long as the ban lasts to prevent ban evasion.
  • Punishment records: for as long as they're relevant to moderation.
  • Donation records: for as long as needed for our records and any legal requirements.

7. Keeping it safe

We limit access to staff who need it, use access controls on our servers and tools, and keep software up to date. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your data we'll let you know when the law requires it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California CCPA), you may have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct data that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your data.
  • Object to or limit how we use your data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional features.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, open a ticket in the Discord (see Contact us). We may need to confirm you own the Minecraft account first, for example by asking you to join the server or run a command. We'll reply within 30 days.

Note that we may keep some data after a deletion request where we need it to enforce an active ban or meet a legal obligation.

9. Children

FemboyMC is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 is playing, contact us and we'll remove their data.

10. This website

This website doesn't use analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising. It's hosted on Cloudflare, which processes basic technical data (like your IP address) to deliver the page and protect it from attacks. The site loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts and cdnjs, which may also see your IP address when your browser requests those files.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date above and announce significant changes in the Discord.

12. Contact us

Questions or privacy requests? Open a ticket in the Femboy Hangout Discord and a staff member will help you.
